Research meets operations.

Sentinel extends TickLab with deterministic replay, data validation, delayed-label scoring and an alert lifecycle. The same clock can be replayed exactly; exported audits include the active rules, observations and incident transitions.

Quality faults are counted cumulatively within a replay day. A corrupted stream therefore stays flagged until reset or a fresh day. Performance and freshness alerts can recover as the rolling window and feed change.

This is an educational simulation on four 20-minute historical slices, not a deployed monitor. No order routing, live data or notifications. Full historical outcomes are bundled for replay; the interface masks them according to availability, not for data security. No Alipes or Binance affiliation.
